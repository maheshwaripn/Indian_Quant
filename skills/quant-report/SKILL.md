Explain final liquidation, unresolved writeoffs, the selected fee model, uncertainty and cost stress. Never call a synthetic high Sharpe evidence of an edge.

Inspect portfolio.csv or predictions.csv only when needed for a specific question. Distinguish missing metrics from passing checks. Record resource usage and zero built-in AI calls; external assistant usage is not measured here.

Recommend the most important next validation step. Do not automatically tune after poor performance or infer profitability from positive IC. Keep research, paper and live labels separate.
